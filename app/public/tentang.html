About Us

Slow bar & roastery for true coffee lovers

carefully curated single origin coffee, matcha, & kombucha in Citraland, West Surabaya

Nordic by The Founders is an artisan specialty coffee slow bar and roastery for true coffee lovers and experience seekers. Founded by two people with over a decade of experience as coffee professionals, trainers, and consultants — every cup is brewed with precision and a love for carefully selected beans.

We curate the finest single origins, quality matcha, and kombucha, and roast our own selected beans (Founders Roast) — also available to take home in tube packaging.
